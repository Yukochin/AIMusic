Step 3 of 4

Students Practice & Record

Students log in from any device, select their piece, and hit record. AI feedback is generated within seconds — no teacher present required.

🎙️
Live Recording

Student opens the AI Teacher on any browser — phone, tablet, or laptop. They select their piece, press Record, and play for up to 90 seconds. Real-time pitch and volume meters appear as they play.

📤
Upload Audio File

If a student has a pre-recorded session on their device, they switch to the Upload tab and drop the file. The AI analyses the audio exactly the same way — with full feedback and scoring.

🤖
Instant AI Feedback

GPT-4o analyses the transcription, pitch accuracy, rhythm and dynamics and returns a structured report: scores out of 10 for each dimension, specific observations, and 3 actionable next steps.

🗓️
AI Practice Planner

After each session, the agentic planner automatically updates the student's 7-day practice plan based on their latest weak areas, piece history, and stated goal — without any teacher input.
