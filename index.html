Platform Features

Everything Your Academy Needs

From real-time AI feedback to school-wide analytics — Inspire covers the full student lifecycle.

🎙️

Real-Time AI Feedback

Students record their practice session. Our AI analyses intonation, rhythm, dynamics, tone quality, and musicality — delivering detailed feedback within seconds, just like a live teacher.

📊

Progress Analytics Dashboard

School administrators and teachers get a bird's-eye view of every student's progress. Identify at-risk learners early, celebrate top performers, and allocate teacher time smarter.

🗓️

AI Practice Planner

The AI agent analyses session history and student goals to auto-generate a personalised weekly practice plan. Keeps students on track between lessons with zero manual effort.

🎓

Curriculum Management

Map repertoire to ABRSM, Trinity, or Suzuki grade levels. Build custom syllabi, assign pieces to cohorts, and track exam preparation milestones across your entire student roster.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Portal & Reports

Automatically generated weekly progress reports delivered to parents. Build transparency, trust, and retention — all without adding administrative overhead to your teaching staff.

🔗

LMS & API Integrations

Connect Inspire to your existing school management system via REST API. Export session reports to CSV or PDF. SSO support for Google Workspace and Microsoft 365.

How It Works

Up and Running in 4 Steps

Onboard your academy in under a day. No technical setup required.

1

Create Your Academy

Register your school, invite teachers, and configure your curriculum and grade structure in minutes.

2

Enrol Students

Add students individually or bulk-import via CSV. Each student gets a secure personal login and practice portal.

3

Students Practice & Record

Students use the AI Teacher tool to record sessions from any device. AI feedback is generated instantly after each session.

4

Track & Report

Administrators and teachers review progress dashboards. Parents receive automated weekly reports. Repeat.
